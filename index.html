Pokcade

Privacy policy

Effective September 16, 2026 · Extension version 0.1.5

Your games stay in your browser. Pokcade stores progress and preferences locally. The extension does not send gameplay data to the developer or third parties, and it includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

What this policy covers

This policy describes the Pokcade Chrome extension, listed as “Pokcade - Popup Games Extension | Over 20+ Games,” and this privacy-policy page. Pokcade is a collection of built-in games that you can play from the Chrome toolbar.

Information stored on your device

Pokcade stores the following information locally in your Chrome browser profile:

  • Game high scores and saved game progress.
  • Your last selected game, menu scroll and focus preferences, and light or dark theme preference.
  • Cookie Clicker production counts, progress, and timestamps used to calculate capped earnings when you return.
  • Whether you have seen the current update announcement.

This information lets you resume games, keep your records and preferences, receive offline progress, and avoid repeated update announcements. It is not sent to the developer or third parties by the extension.

Pokcade uses Chrome’s storage permission for this local information. It also caches your theme preference in the extension’s own local storage. The extension does not synchronize this information to a server or another device.

What the extension does not access

Pokcade does not request your browsing history, website contents, browser cookies, location, personal communications, passwords, or payment information. It has no account system, advertisements, analytics, or tracking services, and it does not make outbound network requests during gameplay.

A local installation/update handler schedules update notes. It does not track browsing, contact a server, or keep games running in the background. Offline earnings are calculated from locally saved timestamps when the game is reopened.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

Pokcade does not sell, transmit, or share its locally stored gameplay information. It keeps that information in your browser profile so you can return to your games.

In-game restart controls reset the relevant game progress; high scores may remain. Cookie Clicker’s confirmed Reset clears that bakery’s progress but preserves its best score. Removing the extension through Chrome removes its extension-local data. Browser and device backups are managed separately by you or your browser or device provider.

External links and this policy website

The optional “Leave a review” link opens Pokcade’s Chrome Web Store reviews page in a new tab. It does not automatically submit a rating, review, or gameplay information. Your use of the Chrome Web Store is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and your Google account settings.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, or third-party fonts, and it does not itself set cookies or use browser storage. GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address, when serving this page, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Visiting this website is separate from playing games in the extension.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if Pokcade’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
